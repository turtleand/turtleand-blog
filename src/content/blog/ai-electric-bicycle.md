An electric bicycle makes the ride smoother. A small push carries us farther, and a difficult hill becomes manageable. AI can feel much the same: it handles a calculation, shapes a draft, or helps us find a useful next step.

There is no need to make every journey harder. Two things still deserve our attention.

- **Keep some pedaling in the ride.** Occasionally reduce the assistance on a small, familiar part of the work. Recall an idea before reading the explanation, or make an estimate before checking the total. [Recalling information can help us retain it](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/). Begin with one step, use a hint when needed, and take on more as understanding grows. This gives the capabilities we value a place to stay in use, without making every task an exercise.
- **Keep the steering.** Assistance can suggest a route. We still decide where to go, whether the route makes sense, and who might be affected. A smooth result does not settle those questions. We can delegate parts of the work while keeping responsibility for direction and consequences.

**A small experiment:** On one familiar task today, try the first step without help. Then bring the help back and compare.
